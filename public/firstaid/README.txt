POSITIONING IMAGES — drop your downloaded first-aid diagrams here.

The app looks for these exact filenames (PNG or SVG both work — if you use
SVG, change the extension in src/data/positionImages.js):

  recovery-position.png   — person on their side, unconscious but breathing
  keep-still.png          — person kept still, do-not-move-neck / spinal care
  bleeding-pressure.png   — direct pressure on a wound
  sitting-up.png          — conscious person sat upright, supported

WHERE TO GET THEM (free, properly licensed):

1. Wikimedia Commons — search "recovery position", "first aid" —
   many are CC BY-SA (free, needs credit). Download the PNG or SVG.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/

2. OpenMoji (openmoji.org) — clean CC BY-SA medical figures.

3. Any first-aid illustration you have the right to use.

ATTRIBUTION: whatever you download, note its author + license in
src/data/positionImages.js (there's a field for it) — the app shows a small
credit line under the images. This is a licence requirement for CC BY-SA, not
optional.

IMPORTANT: pick clear, simple diagrams. A confusing positioning picture is
worse than none for a first-aid app. When in doubt, leave it out — the card
still works with just its text steps.
